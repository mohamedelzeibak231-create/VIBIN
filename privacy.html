Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information vibin ("we", "us") collects when you use our website at vibin.publicvm.com, and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a username, and a password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our login provider and are not visible to us.
  • Sign in with Google or Discord: if you choose these options, we receive basic profile information from them, such as your name, email address and profile name. We do not receive your Google or Discord password.
  • Content you post: videos and captions you upload, and actions such as likes and saves.

How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in.
  • To show your username and content on vibin.
  • To keep the site secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles your data

We use Supabase to handle logins and store account data, and GitHub Pages to host the website. Google and Discord process your data when you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time using the contact email below.

Your choices

  • You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you.
  • You can stop using Google or Discord sign-in by removing vibin's access in your Google or Discord account settings.

Children

vibin is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
