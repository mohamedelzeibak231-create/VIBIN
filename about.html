What you can do on vibin

Post

Upload a short video or a set of photos and add a caption with #hashtags.

Discover

Scroll the For You feed, explore trending posts, and search for people and tags.

Connect

Follow creators, like and comment on posts, save your favourites and message friends.

Stay in control

Make your account private, block people, and delete your account and data any time.

Signing in

You can create an account with your email, or continue with Google or Discord. If you use Google or Discord, vibin only receives your basic profile information (name, email address and profile name), which is used to create and manage your account. We never see your Google or Discord password, and we do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

You can read exactly how your information is handled in our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.
